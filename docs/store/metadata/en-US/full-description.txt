Speeko is a Duolingo-style language-learning app built for learners of Indian languages.

WHAT YOU GET
- Ten language starters plus a Hindi pilot: Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu
- Short A1 lessons (about 8 lessons per language, 23 exercises each) with a shared exercise engine across web and Android
- Multiple exercise kinds: choice, type-the-translation, match pairs, word bank, arrange-the-sentence, listen-and-choose, and speaking practice
- Real practice loops: every correct answer feeds your spaced-repetition queue — due reviews, trouble words, per-unit practice
- Progress that matters: XP, hearts, daily goals, streaks with freezes, daily quests, gems, a friends-free league, and a shop of original fox cosmetics
- Account sync across devices, offline-guest mode that merges when you sign in

AN HONEST ALPHA
Speeko is pre-release software. Every lesson and exercise is marked as a draft and none has been reviewed by a native speaker yet. Playable does not mean correct. If something reads wrong, it is wrong — tell us, and help us fix it.

WHY WE EXIST
Language learning should be approachable for every Indian language community, with content created with, and verified by, native speakers. We build honest software: no fake urgency, no artificial streak pressure dressed as design, rewards that can't be farmed by tapping twice.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
You can learn as a guest. Progress stays on your device and merges when you create an account.

KNOWN GAPS (as of this alpha)
- Content is unreviewed draft material
- Speaking practice is record-yourself only (no automatic scoring)
- Listening exercises may be limited until licensed audio exists
- Full on-device functional QA is still being completed

WHAT WE ASK
If you try Speeko, treat it as a draft: enjoy the flow, flag the mistakes. Native speakers — we need you.
