## Full description (draft)

Speeko is an independent language-learning app for ten Indian languages. It offers short, structured lessons with multiple exercise types, spaced-repetition practice, daily habits and streaks, a shop for cosmetic rewards, and leagues and people for light social motivation.

Features:
- Ten starter courses (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia), each with units and lessons.
- Exercise types: choose the meaning, type the translation, match pairs, listen and choose (audio pending for ten items), word bank, arrange the sentence, type what you hear, and speak (self-reported).
- Spaced-repetition practice that never awards XP.
- Hearts, XP, gems, and a cosmetic shop with server-verified ownership.
- Daily habits, streaks, and goals.
- Leagues and people (follow by username).
- Guest mode: the full learning flow works without an account.
- Account sync: sign in to sync progress across devices.
- In-app license screen (Apache-2.0 code, All Rights Reserved content/brand, OFL fonts).
