**Date:** 2026-11-16, decided 2026-11-18. **Nothing has been published.**

## Why a decision was required

- At the time this package was written, no `LICENSE` file existed. By default,
  all rights were reserved - other developers could **not** legally use Speeko
  source, assets, or content without explicit permission.
- Store listings, public repos, and open collaboration all require an
  explicit grant or reservation of rights.
- The app ships third-party assets under licenses that require attribution.

## Options and practical implications

### Option 1 — MIT OR Apache-2.0 (source open, content not)

- Anyone may use/modify/redistribute **code** with attribution; content and
  brand stay All Rights Reserved.
- Apache-2.0 adds a patent grant (protects users and contributors) while
  keeping obligations light; MIT is simplest and shortest.
- Does not let anyone claim the fox brand or the name spelled "Speeko".
- Fit: open-source adoption; easy for others to build learners/forks.

### Option 2 — AGPL-3.0 (copyleft, network clause)

- Anyone running the app as a network service must also publish their
  changes as AGPL. Protects the commons; few corporate contributors.
- Suits projects wanting hosted-turnkey components to stay open.
- Legal overhead for users; often claimed as "hostile" by enterprises.

### Option 3 — Proprietary / All Rights Reserved (current default)

- Only the owner may use, copy, or distribute; commercial license explicit.
- Prevents remixing by other teachers/developers without permission.
- Store review accepts proprietary apps; no legal obligations to publish.
- Matches the current reality of an unreleased alpha owned by one person.

### Option 4 — Split license (recommended inside option family 1)

- **Code:** Apache-2.0 or MIT (owner's pick).
- **Content + brand assets:** Creative Commons BY-NC (share, credit, not
  for sale) or keep All Rights Reserved — simplest: code open, content
  reserved, attribution for fonts.
- Written into a `LICENSE` (code) + `LICENSE-CONTENT` (content) + `NOTICE`
  (fonts, third-party deps, brand reservation).

## Recommendation

**Option 4, with Apache-2.0 for code (patent grant included), everything
else All Rights Reserved until the owner states otherwise.** This supports
a showing/beta and eventual fork/credit without giving away the curriculum.
If the owner wants the courses in the open too: CC BY-NC 4.0 for content —
never CC BY alone while lessons are unreviewed.

## Required before shipping whatever the owner picks

1. Add `LICENSE`/`NOTICE` text (Apache-2.0 or MIT verbatim; never author a
   custom "do whatever but credit me" clause).
2. Add OFL-1.1 attribution for Nunito/Baloo 2 in `NOTICE` or `docs/`.
3. Add `LICENSE: CONTENT ALL RIGHTS RESERVED` header note in `content/`
   (each course JSON or a `content/README.md`).
4. Add `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` listing fonts, planned open assets, and (if any)
   imported sources pending. Review package keeps the reviewer sign-off.
5. Confirm no code/content was copied from Duolingo/other apps or copied
   Stack Overflow snippets with restrictive licenses.
6. Retain `DISTRO` restrictions: the APK must include `NOTICE` if Apache-2.0.

## What this package does not decide (owner-only)

- Commercial vs free distribution policy.
- Store listing trademark claims.
- Rebranding exposure.
- Interest in accepting external contributions (code review burden).
