# Speeko — Brand Identity

Gamified language learning for Indian scripts. Friendly, vibrant, encouraging, authentically Desi.

## Wordmark

`speeko` in **Nunito Black** (Google Font), lowercase, tight tracking. `speek` in **#16A34A**, final `o` in **#F97316**.

**Hindi:** स्पीको — set alongside or below the Latin wordmark, never styled differently from the Latin.

## Clearspace & size

Clearspace = half the fox-head height on all sides. Minimum size for the mark: 24 px; for the lockup: 96 px wide.

## Typography

- **Nunito Black** — display, headlines, buttons, wordmark
- **Nunito Sans** — body / UI text
- Native scripts render at **17 px+**, always with proper Unicode fonts (`Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Noto Sans Tamil`, etc.)

## The fox

Speeko the fox is the mascot: marigold-orange face, cream cheeks and bib, **bindi dot** on the forehead, café-brown outlines. Two canonical poses:

- **Pose A — the Welcomer.** Waving, onboarding, daily greetings, lesson starts.
- **Pose B - the Listener.** Sitting attentively, quiz/lesson exercises, progress states.
- **Pose C - the Singer.** Head tilted, mouth open, eyes closed in joy, with green musical notes. Brand logo and hero; the web header animates the notes.

Do not redraw the fox off-model; keep the bindi dot, the white double-gradient eye shine, and the stroke weight at 4–5 units on a 100-unit canvas.

## Tone of voice

Encouraging, a little playful, never condescending. Hindi / Indian-English flavour allowed (“Namaste! Ready for lesson one?”, “Sab kaam sahi chal raha hai!”). Microcopy is bilingual whenever a phrase appears in-app.
